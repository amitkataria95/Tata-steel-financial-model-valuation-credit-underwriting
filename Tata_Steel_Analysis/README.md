# Tata Steel Limited — Financial Model, Valuation & Credit Analysis

## Overview

This analysis evaluates Tata Steel Limited (Consolidated) using public financial disclosures and market data. It combines an integrated financial forecast, intrinsic and relative valuation, and a credit downside assessment.

- Debt, interest, minimum-cash and cash-sweep schedule
- FCFF DCF valuation and CAPM-based WACC

## Forecast Methodology

Revenue is forecast using separate volume and realization assumptions:

`Revenue = Deliveries × Revenue per tonne`

Working capital is forecast using operating days:

`Trade Receivables = Revenue × DSO / 365`

`Inventory = Cash Operating Cost × DIO / 365`

`Trade Payables = Cash Operating Cost × DPO / 365`

Capex is separated between maintenance and expansion / transition spending. Expansion expenditure is phased through CWIP before capitalization into PP&E. The debt schedule maintains a minimum cash balance and applies a partial excess-cash sweep rather than assuming that all free cash flow is immediately used for debt reduction.

## Valuation Framework

`FCFF = EBIT × (1 - Tax Rate) + D&A - Capex - Change in NWC`

The analysis includes a CAPM-based WACC, perpetuity-growth and exit-multiple terminal values, 5x5 sensitivity matrices, reverse DCF, EV/EBITDA trading comparables and an EV/tonne cross-check.

## Credit Framework

The credit analysis distinguishes recurring maintenance-basis debt-service capacity from cash coverage after the full expansion programme. Base, severe-recession and liquidity-stress cases assess leverage, interest coverage, DSCR and liquidity requirements.

Proposed lender thresholds include:

- Maximum Net Debt / EBITDA: 3.50x
- Minimum maintenance-basis DSCR: 1.50x
- Minimum EBIT / cash interest: 2.50x
- Minimum cash + committed liquidity: ₹25,000 crore

## Limitations

This analysis uses public information and analyst assumptions. Forecast assumptions, debt amortization proxies, stress parameters and selected valuation inputs are identified in the workbook. A final financing or investment decision would require updated market information, contractual debt terms, facility documentation, detailed project funding schedules and additional due diligence.
